# References

Published work this project builds on, tests against or differs from. Entries are as cited in
the project's documents and pre-registrations; check volume and page details at the publisher
before quoting any of them outside the team. A new study names the closest of these in its card
and says how it differs.

## The earnings volatility effect

- Patell, J. M. and Wolfson, M. A. (1981). The ex ante and ex post price effects of quarterly
  earnings announcements reflected in option and stock prices. *Journal of Accounting
  Research* 19(2), 434-458.
- Dubinsky, A., Johannes, M., Kaeck, A. and Seeger, N. J. (2019). Option pricing of earnings
  announcement risks. *Review of Financial Studies* 32(2). The announcement as a priced jump.
- Gao, C., Xing, Y. and Zhang, X. (2018). Anticipating uncertainty: straddles around earnings
  announcements. *Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis* 53(6). Long straddles into the
  announcement.
- Alexiou, L., Goyal, A., Kostakis, A. and Rompolis, L. (2025). Pricing event risk: evidence
  from concave implied volatility curves. *Review of Finance*.
- Barth, M. E. and So, E. C. (2014). Non-diversifiable volatility risk and risk premiums at
  earnings announcements. *The Accounting Review* 89(5).
- Khan, S. and Khan, Z. (2024). Trading the implied volatility crush after earnings
  announcements. Working paper.

## Option returns and the cross-section

- Goyal, A. and Saretto, A. (2009). Cross-section of option returns and volatility. *Journal of
  Financial Economics* 94(2), 310-326. Implied-minus-realised sorts, concentrated in illiquid
  names.
- Vasquez, A. (2017). Equity volatility term structures and the cross-section of option
  returns. *Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis* 52(6), 2727-2754.
- Bakshi, G. and Kapadia, N. (2003). Delta-hedged gains and the negative market volatility risk
  premium. *Review of Financial Studies* 16(2), 527-566.
- Bakshi, G., Kapadia, N. and Madan, D. (2003). Stock return characteristics, skew laws, and the
  differential pricing of individual equity options. *Review of Financial Studies* 16(1).
- Carr, P. and Wu, L. (2009). Variance risk premiums. *Review of Financial Studies* 22(3),
  1311-1341.
- Bollerslev, T., Tauchen, G. and Zhou, H. (2009). Expected stock returns and variance risk
  premia. *Review of Financial Studies* 22(11), 4463-4492.
- Hann, R. N., Kim, H. and Zheng, Y. (2019). Intra-industry information transfers: evidence
  from changes in implied volatility around earnings announcements. *Review of Accounting
  Studies* 24, 927-971.
- Xing, Y., Zhang, X. and Zhao, R. (2010). What does the individual option volatility smirk tell
  us about future equity returns? *Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis* 45(3).
- Jin, W., Livnat, J. and Zhang, Y. (2012). Option prices leading equity prices: do option
  traders have an information advantage? *Journal of Accounting Research* 50(2).
- Johnson, T. L. and So, E. C. (2018). Time will tell: information in the timing of scheduled
  earnings news. *Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis* 53(6).
- Bernard, V. L. and Thomas, J. K. (1989). Post-earnings-announcement drift: delayed price
  response or risk premium? *Journal of Accounting Research* 27, 1-36.
- McLean, R. D. and Pontiff, J. (2016). Does academic research destroy stock return
  predictability? *Journal of Finance* 71(1).

## Execution and microstructure

- Muravyev, D. and Pearson, N. D. (2020). Options trading costs are lower than you think.
  *Review of Financial Studies* 33(11), 4973-5014.
- Chan, K., Chung, Y. P. and Johnson, H. (1995). The intraday behavior of bid-ask spreads for
  NYSE stocks and CBOE options. *Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis* 30(3). Option
  spreads narrow through the day rather than following the equity U-shape.
- Goyenko, R., Ornthanalai, C. and Tang, S. (2015). Options illiquidity: determinants and
  implications for stock returns. Working paper. Spreads widen into earnings. (Cited in project
  notes as "Goyenko et al. 2015"; co-authors and title are recalled, not verified.)
