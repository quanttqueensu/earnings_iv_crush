# Entry-spread filter on DoltHub 2019-2024: not confirmed

Pre-registration `_private/entry_spread_dolthub_prereg.md`. The hypothesis was generated by
exploring the 2025-2026 broad block after a failed test (tight entry-spread tercile +1.69% of margin
at the touch); this was its one test on data that played no part in finding it. Threshold fixed at an
entry half-spread of 3.82% of mid. Script: `scripts/research_entry_spread_dolthub.py`.

1,421 events, 64 names; 1,121 scored (39 empty chains, 261 exit straddles unquoted at the stored
strike). Short straddle sold at the entry-date bid, bought at the exit-date ask, DoltHub end-of-day
quotes, nearest listed expiry after exit.

| book | N | touch net per trade | Sharpe [CI] | hit | gross at mid | median entry half-spread |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| filtered | 340 | -1.80% | -0.324 [-0.399, -0.251] | 0.182 | -0.13% | 2.8% |
| unfiltered | 1,121 | -4.21% | -0.634 [-0.694, -0.581] | 0.118 | +0.13% | 5.2% |
| above threshold | 781 | -5.26% | -0.773 [-0.856, -0.697] | 0.090 | +0.24% | 7.2% |
| filtered, megacap | 58 | -1.70% | -0.307 [-0.520, -0.069] | 0.172 | -0.27% | 1.7% |
| filtered, non-megacap | 282 | -1.82% | -0.327 [-0.413, -0.253] | 0.184 | -0.10% | 2.9% |

**Primary criterion fails; mechanism check fails** (the filtered book's gross premium is not
preserved). The exploratory finding is not confirmed and carries no weight.

A limitation that weakens the test without reversing it: DoltHub lists no weekly expiries, so the
nearest expiry after the event is a median 16 days from entry (10 to 24), against 8 days on the
megacap book. The event is a smaller share of a longer option's value and the gross premium at the
mid is approximately zero in every book, so there was little premium for a cost filter to preserve.
The cost ordering itself holds: tight-spread events lose less than wide ones at the touch.
