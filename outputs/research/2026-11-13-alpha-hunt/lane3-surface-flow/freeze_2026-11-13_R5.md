# Freeze note, 2026-11-13: R5 event richness against own history

Written before any held-out outcome is read. One candidate is taken forward.

**Hypothesis.** Events whose implied event move is high against the name's own realised
earnings moves keep more of the premium on the next-day buy-back.

**Mechanism.** With the diffusive part of front variance stripped (two-expiry jump split), an
implied event move above the name's own history is premium the event does not earn back, so the
gap cost per unit of premium is lower. Discovery decomposition agrees: the lift is carried by gap
cost (-0.071 of premium [-0.137, -0.005]), not by residual extrinsic value (+0.003).

**Specification (identical to trial 1,694).** Feature `rich_event` = 0.5 log(jsplit_jump_var)
minus the mean of log max(|move|, 0.001) over the name's last eight events (at least four) whose
exit precedes entry. Stamped 15:59 ET on the entry session. Treated = top causal tercile, cuts
from strictly earlier announcement dates, minimum 200 prior events. Outcome = net buy-back P&L
per unit of premium at the measured cost (1.2714% in, 3.0182% out, exit at the ask when no
two-sided mid), compared within cells of causal implied-move tercile by tenor bucket. Intervals
by date-clustered bootstrap, 5,000 draws, seed 0. Null = per-trade Sharpe on margin of the treated
set against count-matched random draws within cell.

**Blocks.** Discovery 2016-19 (scored: +0.0707 [+0.0100, +0.1309], t +2.29, null percentile 99.3,
N 1,615, 494 treated). Held-out 2020-24, read once (trial 1,707). 2025-26 read once as a
secondary block (trial 1,708).

**Pass rule.** Held-out interval above zero, t at least 3, null percentile at least 95.

**Label whatever the outcome.** Exploratory. The block ledger shows 2016-24 and 2025-26 already
viewed for the buy-back outcome with own-history and term-structure-jump features (trials 1,623
to 1,628), so neither block is clean for this family, and discovery itself fell short of t 3.
Nearest closed study: `selection/2026-10-20-jump-richness` (same family, scored on residual
value, 2013-18, failed). This version differs in the outcome (net buy-back per unit premium,
which contains gap cost, the driver named here) and in the tenor-matched cells.
