# Freeze note, lane 4 (structure), 2026-11-13

Written after the discovery read (broad events entered in 2025, N=801) and before any outcome of
the broad events entered in 2026 (N=419) was computed in this lane. The megacap 2023-24 tick block
(N=243) was scored in the same pass as discovery as a cross-universe check, so it is not held out.

Block-ledger position: `research/ledger/blocks.csv` records the whole 2025-26 broad panel as viewed
descriptively for the entry clock and for all outcome families. No block is clean for these
hypotheses, so whatever the held-out read shows is labelled exploratory, not confirmatory.

## Candidate 1 (primary): touch-only clock stack, trial 1,727

Sell both legs at the 15:45:00 bids on the entry day (15:59:00 bids if either leg is not two-sided
at 15:45) and buy both back at the 15:30:00 asks on the reaction day (15:59:00 asks if a 15:30 ask
is missing), same strike and expiry as the base book. Base B0: 15:59:00 bids and 15:59:00 asks.
Statistic: mean paired difference TC minus B0 per event, fraction of the common margin
m0 = 0.20 x parity forward + 15:59 bid premium, net of USD 0.65 per contract per leg per side.
Pass: date-clustered 95% interval above zero, clustered t at least 3, date-cluster sign-flip null
percentile at least 95. Secondary, reported whatever it shows: the level of TC (is the book net
positive), by half-spread tercile.

Mechanism stated in advance: broad-name option quotes widen into the close on both days, so a
fixed-clock taker pays less spread earlier; the mid does not move against the seller between the
clocks. Refuted if the gain is absent in 2026 or is carried by a mid drift rather than the spread.

## Candidate 2: stacked book with package execution at a fill haircut, trial 1,724

Entry as one package at the 15:45 mid less 0.391 of the event's own 15:45 half-spread; exit as one
package at the 15:59 mid plus 0.384 of the exit half-spread (touch when the exit quote is one-sided).
Both shares are the discovery-block means over events that had a late package print (187 entry
events, 46 exit events of 801) and are applied to every event, which assumes that events without a
print would trade at the same share of their own, wider, spread. Fill probability is not measured:
the frozen statistic is the 50% fill arm (half the stack, half B0), with fill probabilities 1 and
0.25 beside it. Pass rule as for candidate 1, with the same caveat that the level is an assumption
about fills, not a measurement.
