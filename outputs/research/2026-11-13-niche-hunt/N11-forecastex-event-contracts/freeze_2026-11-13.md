# Freeze note: N11 ForecastEx pilot, held-out read

Written 2026-11-13, after discovery trials 2,382 to 2,387 and before any held-out outcome was
loaded. The held-out block is releases from 2026-01-01 to the end of the downloaded data. The
only held-out quantities computed before this note are bet counts with outcomes blinded (H1
single-signal 21 bets on 12 dates; every-print signals 628 bets on 58 contracts and 19 dates).
The held-out scorer is `scripts/score_heldout.py`; it verifies every hash below, runs the frozen
configurations once and applies these rules.

## Frozen configurations

- Common: ForecastEx fee 0.01 per contract, adverse allowance a = 0.02 against the fill print,
  fills capped at 0.99, held to resolution, outcome from the ForecastEx settlement on the
  expiration date, intervals date-clustered by release date (5,000 draws, seed 0, stat mean).
- H1, trial 2,386 (primary): every ForecastEx print in (R - 24 h, R - 1 h] on FFDEC, FF, CPIY,
  CPIC, UNR, PREMP contracts is a signal when |Kalshi twin mid - print| >= 0.04 (mid from the
  last two-sided hourly candle ending at or before the print, at most 2 h old); fill at the next
  print on the contract before R - 5 min; P&L averaged within contract, then the interval over
  contracts clustered by release date.
- H1, trial 2,387 (support): one bet per contract, signal at the first print in the window,
  fill at the next print.
- H2, trial 2,385: favourite side when the first print in the window is 0.90 or more (YES) or
  0.10 or less (NO), on FFDEC, FF, CPIY, CPIC, UNR, PREMP, IJC, PCEY; fill at the next print.

## Pass rules

- H1 passes only if the 2,386 contract-averaged mean has a lower bound above zero with at least
  30 contracts on at least 10 release dates, and the 2,387 single-signal mean is above zero.
  Mechanism reported beside it: 2,387 gross gap capture and Brier(ForecastEx) minus
  Brier(Kalshi); a pass with a non-positive gap capture is reported as unexplained.
- H2 passes if the 2,385 mean has a lower bound above zero with at least 30 bets on at least 10
  release dates.
- Anything else is a fail (or underpowered where the counts fall short). Sensitivities at a =
  0.01 and 0.03 are reported, not used.
