# Freeze note: E1 reaction-hour long gamma scalp (trial 2,682)

Written 2026-11-14 after the discovery read and before any held-out outcome. The held-out scorer
`heldout.py` was written before this note; its hash is below.

## What is frozen

The declared primary, unchanged from diagnosis.log entry 2: buy the held straddle (the 15:59 entry
strike, front expiry) at the 15:01:00 ET ask on the reaction session, delta-hedge in the stock on a
5-minute clock with the hedge ratio from the parity forward and straddle-mid implied vol observed
30 seconds earlier, sell at the 15:59:00 bid. Option legs pay the quoted touch plus USD 0.65 per
contract per leg; stock legs pay an assumed 3 bp half-spread plus 1 bp of traded notional.
Statistic: mean net P&L per trade in % of the debit, date-clustered 95% interval over reaction
dates. Pass: mean above zero with the interval excluding zero. Secondary, descriptive only: the
same mean in % of m0, the gross-at-mid control C1 and the mean round-trip option touch.

## Why the primary and not a variant

Nothing passed discovery. The primary failed at -11.66% of debit [-13.01, -10.24], N=578 on 193
reaction dates (t about -16.5), and its kill rule fired: the gross at the mid, +0.86%
[+0.46, +1.26], is about a twelfth of the mean round-trip option touch, 10.57%. No variant came
within ten points of zero, so freezing a variant would select on noise. The held-out read
measures whether that cost structure holds on unseen events; no held-out result can turn the
trial into a finding.
