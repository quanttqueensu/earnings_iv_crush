# Pre-registration: entry-spread filter on a fresh block (DoltHub 2019-2024)

Frozen before any DoltHub quote for this test is pulled. SHA-256 recorded in
`outputs/research/prereg_entry_spread_dolthub.json`.

## Status of the hypothesis, stated plainly

This hypothesis was **generated by exploration**, not deduced in advance. After T1 of
`final_earnings_set_prereg.md` failed, the 2025-2026 broad block (already viewed many times) was
sorted by the entry half-spread, which is observable at entry. The tightest tercile (entry
half-spread at most 3.82% of mid, N=407) returned +1.69% of margin per trade at the quoted touch,
per-trade Sharpe +0.116, date-clustered CI [+0.008, +0.239]; the middle and wide terciles lost
(-0.117 and -0.341). Gross premium at the mid was similar across terciles (+3.6%, +2.7%, +4.0%),
so the difference is the toll. The tercile boundary was chosen on that sample. The result counts
for nothing until it reproduces on data that played no part in finding it.

The strongest counter-evidence is already on file: megacap events have entry spreads in the same
range and lose at the touch (per-trade Sharpe -0.059 and -0.092 in 2019-2024 and 2013-2018,
`cost_levers/spread_gate.csv`) because their gross premium is about +0.5% of margin, not +3.6%.

## Mechanism

The announcement premium is roughly uniform across spread levels in gross terms; a liquidity taker
keeps it only where the round-trip toll is smaller than the premium. A fixed entry-spread ceiling
selects those events using information available at entry.

## Block

`outputs/research/dolthub_sp500_events.parquet`: 1,421 events, 64 names, entries 2019-02-12 to
2024-11-14. Quotes are DoltHub end-of-day bid and ask (the publisher's snapshot, not 15:59 ET),
a different source and time stamp from every block used before. Megacap names are kept in, not
removed, so the known counter-evidence is part of the test; the megacap and non-megacap split is
reported.

## Trade

Sell the straddle at the event table's stored strike on the nearest listed expiry on or after the
exit date, at the entry-date bid; buy it back at the exit-date ask. One contract; margin
`0.20 * spot_entry + entry premium at the mid`, per share, times 100. Events missing either quote
are dropped and counted.

## Rule

Trade if `(entry_ask - entry_bid) / (entry_ask + entry_bid) <= 0.0382`. Fixed; not re-estimated.

## Success criterion

The filtered book's per-trade Sharpe of touch-basis return on margin has a date-clustered 95% CI
(5,000 draws, seed 0) excluding zero and positive. **Mechanism check**: the filtered book's mean
touch return exceeds the unfiltered book's, and its gross return at the mid is not materially lower
(within one standard error) than the unfiltered book's. One trial; DSR against 1,495.
