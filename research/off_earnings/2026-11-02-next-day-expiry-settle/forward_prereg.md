# Forward pre-registration: next-day-expiry index straddle, settled

Frozen before any forward observation exists. Record:
`outputs/research/prereg_2026-11-02-next-day-expiry-settle-forward.json`. Generated by stream 17
(frozen FAIL at a 5 bp disposal; positive in every SPY block at 0.5 to 1 bp, a cost chosen after the
outcome). This text fixes the rule and the cost before data that could confirm or refute it.

## Rule

Every NYSE session t on which SPY, QQQ and IWM each list an expiry on the next session t+1:

1. At 15:59 ET read the NBBO of the t+1-expiry chain (real-time quotes only; delayed quotes void the
   observation, counted).
2. Strike: two-sided pairs only, minimising `|C_mid - P_mid|`.
3. Sell one call and one put at their bids (the forward record uses the 15:59 bids as the fill
   whether or not a paper order is placed).
4. No action on t+1 before the close. Settle at intrinsic on the official t+1 close.
5. Assigned or exercised ETF shares are sold at the t+2 open; the record charges 1 bp of the t+1
   close per share delivered plus the actual t+1-close-to-t+2-open move on those shares.
6. Commission USD 0.65 per contract on the two entry legs.

Unit: per trade, fraction of the entry mid premium.

## Criterion

After 250 sessions per ticker (about one year): PASS on SPY if the mean net per trade is > 0 with a
circular block bootstrap (10 sessions, 5,000 draws, seed 0) interval excluding zero. QQQ and IWM are
reported and cannot rescue SPY. Futility: after 125 sessions, stop if the SPY upper bound is below 0.

## Expected power (stated now)

Backtest per-trade sd about 0.65 of premium for SPY; 250 sessions give a half-width of about 8
points of premium against an expected +1% to +4%. The forward year is therefore likely to be
inconclusive on its own; it is a check that the sign survives real quotes and the disposal path,
not a stand-alone proof.
