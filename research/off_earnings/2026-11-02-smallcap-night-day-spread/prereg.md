# Pre-registration: small-cap night-minus-day spread (opening-auction buying pressure)

Frozen before any of the confirmation data below is computed. Record:
`outputs/research/prereg_2026-11-02-smallcap-night-day-spread.json`. Scorer:
`research/off_earnings/2026-11-02-smallcap-night-day-spread/study.py`.

## Origin, stated plainly

Generated by a descriptive look after trial 1,566 (`2026-11-02-overnight-drift-inventory`,
FAIL): on IWM 2016-01 to 2026-10 the overnight leg (close to next open, 1 bp round trip) earned
about 12.6% a year at a daily-series Sharpe of 0.89 against buy-and-hold 0.55, and the intraday
leg (open to close) lost about 5.3% a year. That window is seen and cannot confirm itself. Every
criterion below is scored on data not yet computed in this project.

## Hypothesis

Attention-driven buying concentrates at the open and pushes opening prices above value, which
reverses during the session; the effect is strongest in small, retail-held stocks (Berkman, Koch,
Tuttle and Zhang 2012, JFQA, "Paying attention: overnight returns and the hidden cost of buying
at the open"; Lou, Polk and Skouras 2019, JFE). Hypothesis: for small-cap equity ETFs, a book
long from the close auction to the next opening auction and short from the opening auction to
the close auction earns a positive mean net.

Closest closed rows: none on underlying overnight/intraday returns; trial 1,566 (index overnight
drift conditional on intraday selling) failed its selector, and its unconditional legs are the
seen data named above.

## Data

Yahoo daily, `auto_adjust=False`, with actions. For session t with previous session t-1:
`ON_t = (O_t + D_t) / C_{t-1} - 1` (dividend with ex-date t), `ID_t = C_t / O_t - 1`. Sessions
with a split on t, or a missing or non-positive open or close, are dropped and counted.

Spread per session: `S_t = ON_t - ID_t - 2c`, where c is the round-trip cost per leg: 1 bp for
IWM, 2 bp for the basket ETFs (thinner auctions). Unit: fraction of notional per session; the
book holds one unit of notional long overnight and one unit short intraday.

## Criterion (all three on unseen data)

1. **IWM 2001-01-02 to 2015-12-31**: mean `S_t` > 0 with the circular block bootstrap interval
   (10 sessions, 5,000 draws, seed 0) excluding zero.
2. **Basket 2016-01-04 to 2026-10-30**: equal-weight daily average of `S_t` over IJR, VB, IWN,
   IWO, VTWO (each ETF enters on sessions it has data; a session needs at least three ETFs):
   mean > 0 with the same interval excluding zero.
3. **Opening-print check, IWM 2016-01-04 to 2026-10-30**: replace `O_t` by the 10:00 parity
   forward from `data/cache/index_chains/IWM_{t}_1000.parquet` (nearest expiry, two-sided pairs,
   strike minimising `|C_mid - P_mid|`, `F = K + C_mid - P_mid`); the resulting spread mean is
   > 0. This guards against an artefact of the opening print; it is not out of sample for the
   level.

PASS if 1, 2 and 3 all hold. Otherwise FAIL.

## Secondary (descriptive)

Each leg separately; per-ETF results; sub-periods 2001-07, 2008-15; costs 0 and 2x; daily-series
Sharpe annualised by the square root of 252 (the book trades every session, so this is its own
cadence); maximum drawdown; correlation of `S_t` with the same-day SPY close-to-close return.
