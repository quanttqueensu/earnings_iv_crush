## Why it matters

If retail demand bids up straddles on names with salient disagreement while the disagreement
partly reflects analysts' models rather than news, the seller would be paid for uncertainty that
does not resolve. It was the only zero-spend idea from the 2026-11-07 pivot slate whose data were
all on disk.
